Research

Development Zone Policies, Industrial Agglomeration, and Household Income: Evidence from China Revised & Resubmit · JRS

Examines how China's development zone policies interact with the hukou system to produce stratified distributional outcomes. Staggered DID, PSM-DID, event study, and Honest-DID sensitivity analysis on 2000–2017 county-level panel data.

Agglomeration Dividend or Cost? A Perspective from the Hukou Restrictions on Migrant Population Revised & Resubmit · HSSC

Identifies the net effect of population density on household consumption and shows that hukou restrictions systematically amplify its negative impact. Two-way fixed effects and IV estimation on 2003–2022 prefecture-level panel data. [Preprint]

Generative AI and Skill Diversity in the Labour Market Working Paper

Uses ChatGPT's 2022 launch as a quasi-natural experiment to study how generative AI reshapes skill composition across Chinese urban labor markets, with PPMI-based skill co-occurrence measures and a city × occupation × month panel.

Regional Development: Both the Creator and Acceptor of Light Pollution ICM 2023 · Meritorious Winner (Top 13%)

Proposed PhD Research

Generative AI, Skill Diversity, and Wage Inequality: A Computational Sociological Study of China's Urban Labor Markets. Exploits ChatGPT's 2022 launch as a quasi-natural experiment to investigate how generative AI restructures skill composition and wage inequality across 170 Chinese cities. Constructs novel PPMI-based skill co-occurrence measures of within- and between-occupation skill diversity, integrating economic task models with sociological theories of occupational closure and the labor process. Empirical strategy: continuous-treatment DID, mediation analysis, and quantile regression on a city × occupation × month panel (~120,000 observations; Python pipeline complete).
